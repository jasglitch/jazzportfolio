About

The short version.

I've spent the last decade at the intersection of consumer behaviour and product systems — most recently as Lead Product Manager at FITPASS, India's largest fitness network, where I built the product team and set up the agile practices that streamline how we ship. I own three domains — Customer App, Partner App, and Corporate Alliances — and drive the full funnel from acquisition to activation, engagement, and monetisation.

My work lives in the numbers: activation rates, redemption journeys, engagement loops, retention curves. But the craft is in translating complex human behaviour into clean, scalable product decisions. Before FITPASS, I worked on quick-commerce for Tier 2/3 India at Fraazo, and on enterprise-scale consumer safety products at Microsoft — two very different worlds that sharpened the same muscle: understanding users who don't behave the way you'd expect.

The Approach

Three things I keep coming back to.

Growth

Funnel optimisation and acquisition experiments that compound — finding the smallest change with the largest downstream effect.

Engagement & Retention

Gamification, lifecycle design, and community features that turn a single visit into a returning habit.

Monetisation

Upsell, cross-sell, and upgrade paths woven into the journey — revenue that feels earned, not extracted.
